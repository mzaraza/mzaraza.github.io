ABOUT

Geospatial engineering with scientific depth and operational focus.

I am a Cadastral and Geodesist Engineer and geospatial specialist with more than eight years of professional experience in Earth Observation, Remote Sensing, GIS, Geospatial AI and Cloud Computing.

My work combines satellite imagery, Machine Learning, cloud computing, geospatial processing and spatial data engineering for national-scale land-cover mapping, agricultural monitoring, mining oversight, environmental analysis and geospatial applications.

I am currently completing the Erasmus Mundus MSc in Geospatial Technologies and developing research on Earth Observation foundation models and satellite image time series.
